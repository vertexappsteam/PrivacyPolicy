## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **CodeSprint** ("we," "our," or "us"). CodeSprint is a mobile application designed to help developers master programming skills through structured 7-day learning sprints, quizzes, and interview preparation exercises.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Android application. Please read this policy carefully. If you disagree with its terms, please discontinue use of the application.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide

When you sign in to CodeSprint using **Google Sign-In**, we receive and store the following from your Google account:

- **Display name** – used to personalize your in-app experience
- **Email address** – used to identify your account
- **Profile photo URL** – used to display your avatar (if you have not selected a custom in-app avatar)

We do **not** receive or store your Google account password.

### 2.2 Information We Create and Store

As you use the app, we create and store the following data in **Google Firebase Firestore** (a cloud database):

| Data Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| User UID | Unique identifier linked to your Google account |
| Display name (custom) | Personalised username you may set in the app |
| Selected avatar | In-app avatar preference |
| XP (experience points) | Tracks learning progress and achievements |
| Streak | Tracks consecutive days of learning |
| Course progress | Which days and tutorials you have completed |
| Completed tutorials | List of completed lesson identifiers |
| Unlocked days | Days unlocked via rewarded ads or completion |
| Account creation timestamp | Records when your account was created |

### 2.3 Automatically Collected Information

When you use CodeSprint, certain information is collected automatically by third-party services integrated into the app:

#### Google AdMob (Advertising)
CodeSprint displays **banner advertisements** and **rewarded video advertisements** provided by **Google AdMob** (operated by Google LLC). AdMob may collect:

- **Advertising ID** (Android Advertising ID / GAID)
- **Device information** (model, operating system version)
- **IP address** (to estimate general location for ad targeting)
- **App usage data** (ad impressions, clicks, interaction metrics)
- **Coarse location data** (for personalised advertising, where permitted)

AdMob uses this data to serve relevant advertisements. You can opt out of personalised ads by resetting your Android Advertising ID or opting out via your device's **Settings → Privacy → Ads**. AdMob's privacy practices are governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

- **Account management** – To create and maintain your user profile
- **Personalisation** – To display your name, avatar, XP, and streak
- **Progress tracking** – To save and restore your course progress across devices
- **Advertising** – To display banner and rewarded ads via Google AdMob in order to fund the free content available in the app
- **App functionality** – To unlock course days via rewarded ad viewing
- **Analytics** – To understand how the app is used and improve the user experience
- **Communications** – We do **not** send marketing emails or push notifications unless you explicitly opt in

We do **not** sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

We share information only in the following limited circumstances:

| Recipient | Data Shared | Purpose |

## 5. Data Retention

We retain your account data (profile, progress, XP, streak) for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with the service.

If you wish to delete your account and all associated data, please contact us at **[vertexappsteam@gmail.com]**. We will process deletion requests within **30 days**.

## 6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

- All data stored in **Firebase Firestore** is encrypted at rest and in transit using TLS
- Access to your Firestore data is governed by **Firebase Security Rules**, which restrict read/write access to your own authenticated user data only
- Google Sign-In uses OAuth 2.0, meaning we never handle or store your Google password

Despite these measures, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

- **Access** – Request a copy of the data we hold about you
- **Correction** – Request correction of inaccurate data
- **Deletion** – Request deletion of your account and associated data
- **Portability** – Request your data in a portable format
- **Opt-out of personalised ads** – Via your Android device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at **[vertexappsteam@gmail.com]**.

### GDPR (European Users)
If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Our lawful basis for processing your data is:
- **Contractual necessity** – To provide the app's core functionality (account, progress)
- **Legitimate interests** – Analytics to improve the app
- **Consent** – For personalised advertising (which you may withdraw at any time)

### CCPA (California Users)
If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, the right to delete it, and the right to opt out of its sale. We do **not** sell personal information.
