Privacy Policy for Scan Nova – QR & Barcode

Last Updated: August 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Scan Nova – QR & Barcode ("Scan Nova", "we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use our mobile application.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. Scan Nova is designed to provide QR code and barcode scanning, QR code creation, scan history, and related functionality while minimizing the information we collect.

By downloading or using Scan Nova, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Scan Nova may process certain information to provide its features.

1.1 Camera Access

Scan Nova requires access to your device's camera to scan QR codes and barcodes.

Camera access is used only when you activate the scanning functionality.

We use the camera to:

Scan QR codes
Scan barcodes
Perform batch scanning
Assist with scanning in low-light conditions using the flashlight

Scan Nova does not use the camera to continuously monitor you or record video in the background.

Camera images/video used for scanning are processed for the purpose of detecting and decoding QR codes or barcodes.

2. Photos and Gallery Access

Scan Nova may allow you to select an image from your device gallery to scan a QR code or barcode contained in that image.

Gallery access is used only when you choose to use the gallery scanning feature.

We do not automatically access your entire photo library.

Images selected for scanning are processed to detect and decode supported QR codes or barcodes.

Unless otherwise stated in this Privacy Policy, we do not upload your selected images to our servers.

3. QR Codes and Barcode Information

When you scan a QR code or barcode, the application may process the information encoded within the code.

Depending on the type of code, this information may include:

Website URLs
Text
Product information
Wi-Fi information
Contact information
Email addresses
Telephone numbers
Other information encoded in the QR code or barcode

The content of a QR code is determined by the person or organization that created the code.

Scan Nova does not control or take responsibility for the information contained in third-party QR codes or barcodes.

4. Scan History

Scan Nova may provide a scan history feature that allows you to review previously scanned codes.

Depending on the version and configuration of the application, scan history may be stored locally on your device.

If scan history is stored locally:

It remains on your device.
We do not automatically receive your scan history.
You can delete stored scan history using the application's available controls or by removing the application.

If a future version of Scan Nova introduces cloud synchronization or account-based storage, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

5. QR Code Creation

Scan Nova allows you to create QR codes from information such as text or URLs.

Information that you enter into the QR code creation feature may be processed locally on your device to generate the QR code.

We do not require you to provide personal information merely to create a QR code.

6. Internet and Website Links

When a scanned QR code contains a website URL, Scan Nova may allow you to open that URL in your browser.

When you choose to open an external website:

The website is controlled by its respective operator.
That website may collect information according to its own privacy policy.
Scan Nova is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any website you visit through a scanned QR code.
