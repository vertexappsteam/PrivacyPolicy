
Privacy Policy for RewardX
Effective Date: 17 May 2026
Last Updated: 17 May 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how RewardX ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the RewardX mobile application (the "App", package name com.rewardX.vertexapp.app) available on Google Play.

By installing or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Information We Collect
1.1 Information You Provide Directly
Google Account information when you sign in: your email address, display name, profile photo URL, and Google account user ID.
Profile preferences: chosen avatar, edited display name.
Bitcoin wallet address that you enter to receive withdrawal payouts.
Referral code that you enter when joining (if invited by another user).
1.2 Information Generated by Your Use of the App
In-app activity: coins earned, ads watched, scratch-card / spin-wheel / quiz / mini-game plays, daily check-ins, social-share actions, and timestamps of these events.
Transaction history: every credit, debit, and withdrawal request linked to your account.
Withdrawal records: amount, destination Bitcoin address, status (pending / processing / completed / failed), and (when applicable) the blockchain transaction hash.
Referral data: your referral code, who referred you, who you have referred.
1.3 Information Collected Automatically
Device & technical data: device model, manufacturer, operating system version, app version, language, time zone, IP address, advertising ID, and crash logs.
Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token used to deliver push notifications.
Approximate location may be inferred by AdMob/Firebase from IP address (we do not collect precise GPS location).
1.4 Information from Advertising Partners
We use Google AdMob to serve rewarded video, interstitial, and banner ads. AdMob collects information including your advertising ID, IP address, ad interaction events, and device characteristics in order to serve, measure, and limit the frequency of ads. See Google's policies linked in Section 4.

2. How We Use Your Information
We use the information we collect to:

Create and authenticate your account.
Track your coin balance, lifetime earnings, daily limits, transactions, and referrals.
Process Bitcoin withdrawal requests and credit referrer rewards.
Show advertisements and credit you with coins when you complete a rewarded ad.
Send push notifications about rewards, withdrawals, announcements, and account status.
Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, and policy violations (e.g. self-referral, multi-account abuse, fraudulent withdrawals).
Diagnose crashes and improve App stability and performance.
Comply with legal obligations, respond to lawful requests, and enforce our terms.
We do not sell your personal information.

6. Bitcoin Withdrawals
When you request a withdrawal, we store your destination Bitcoin address, the amount, and the resulting blockchain transaction hash (once processed). Bitcoin transactions are recorded on the public Bitcoin blockchain and are inherently public, immutable, and outside our control. Anyone with the transaction hash or your wallet address can view those records on a blockchain explorer.

We do not custody, hold, or have access to the cryptocurrency in your wallet beyond sending the payout amount you request.

We share information only:

With the third-party services listed in Section 4, strictly to operate the features they power.
With administrators of the App (internal staff) for account management, fraud prevention, and customer support. Administrators can view your email, display name, coin balance, transaction history, bitcoin address, and withdrawal status through an internal admin panel.
When required by law, legal process, subpoena, or government request.
To protect the rights, safety, or property of RewardX, our users, or the public.
In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets (you will be notified before your information is transferred to a different policy).
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their marketing.

8. Data Retention
Account data is retained for as long as your account exists.
Transaction and withdrawal records are retained for at least 5 years after the transaction, to comply with anti-fraud and accounting obligations.
Crash and analytics data is retained according to Firebase default retention periods (up to 14 months for analytics).
When you request account deletion, we delete or anonymize your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.
